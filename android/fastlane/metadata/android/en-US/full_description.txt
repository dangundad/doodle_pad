Doodle Pad is a simple drawing app for quick sketches, playful doodles, visual notes, and everyday ideas. Open a clean canvas, choose a brush, pick a color, and start drawing right away.

Use it on the sofa, in a meeting, or whenever an idea shows up. The app keeps the drawing experience light and friendly, so you can focus on the moment instead of complicated menus.

✨ Key features

🎨 10 brushes for every mood
• Pen, pencil, marker, brush, highlighter, and fountain pen
• Crayon, watercolor, and airbrush for softer, textured styles
• Eraser for quick touch-ups
• Stroke width that follows the speed of your finger

🖌️ Easy creative controls
• 16 ready-to-use colors plus a custom color picker with hex input
• Brush size slider and canvas background colors
• Undo and redo when you want to adjust your drawing
• Shake your phone to clear the canvas

📷 Draw on your own photos
• Bring in a picture from your gallery and draw on top of it
• Trace an outline, mark up a photo, or add doodles to a snapshot
• The photo is kept with your artwork, so you can reopen and keep editing

💾 Save and come back later
• Save to your device gallery as PNG or JPEG at 1x, 2x, or 3x resolution
• Keep drawings in the in-app artwork gallery and reopen them anytime to keep drawing
• Share finished drawings with messaging apps, social apps, or other Android share options

💎 Optional Premium
• Use the app for free with ads
• Remove ads with a one-time purchase
• Unlock the watercolor and airbrush brushes without watching an ad
• Restore your purchase at any time
